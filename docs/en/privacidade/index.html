Privacy and cookies

Updated on November 4, 2026.

Summary

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools such as Google Analytics. What we call cookies here is, in practice, your browser's storage: small pieces of information kept on your device.

Always on (necessary)

  • Your choice of terms and cookies (hdmi.consent).
  • Light or dark theme (hdmi.theme).
  • Chosen language (techmeow.lang), which applies to all techmeow.app websites.
  • Page transition effect (hdmi.pt), which disappears when you close the tab.

Optional (only with Accept all)

  • Last ad seen, to show a different one on your next visit, and which ads this browser has already seen (hdmi-ad-*).
  • The website you came from (hdmi-de), which disappears when you close the tab.
  • Ad counting, explained below.

If you choose only the necessary

Ads still appear, but views and clicks are not counted, and the optional items are not saved. Changing your choice deletes the optional items from this browser.

Ad counting

With your permission, we tell our server when an ad appears or is clicked, in which place of the website and which website you came from. The server adds up these notices per day, with approximate city and country and device type. We do not store your IP or create an identifier for you.

The program

PS5 HDMI Tool runs on your computer. NOR backups stay on your machine and are not sent to us. The program only checks GitHub to know whether a new version exists.

Services used

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The ad list and ad files come from Vercel and Supabase. As with any website, these services receive your IP and technical data to deliver the content. Mercado Livre, Shopee, WhatsApp, Instagram and YouTube only receive data if you click their links.

Your rights

You can change your choice at any time under Manage cookies, at the bottom of the pages, and request information under Brazil's data protection law (LGPD). Controller: MeoWDeV MOD (Jefferson Honorio). Contact: WhatsApp +55 21 96654-7296.
